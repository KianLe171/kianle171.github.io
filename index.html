About

Data with a purpose

I'm Khoa Tran Huu Le, known as Kian, an economics and public policy researcher based in Bangkok. My path began with a High Distinction degree in Investment Economics at the University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City (UEH) and now combines an MSc in Business Analytics & Digital Transformation at the Asian Institute of Technology, supported by His Majesty the King's Scholarship of the Royal Thai Government, with an MA in Public Policy at O.P. Jindal Global University.

What ties these together is one question: who is being left behind, and what would change that? I apply econometrics, data science and analytics to social impact, child welfare and sustainable development, from the link between household digital literacy and clean energy adoption in Vietnam to how climate and health shocks relate to household poverty.

My master's research now looks at governance readiness for child online safety in Thailand's digital economy, including risks from AI-generated abuse material and deepfake-enabled exploitation. I'm looking to contribute to NGOs, UN agencies and international development organizations where rigorous analysis helps protect children and vulnerable communities.

Research ACBES 2023 · Aug 2023

Impact of Digital Literacy on Vietnamese Households' Clean Energy Consumption

Presented at the 5th Asia Conference on Business and Economics, organized by the Journal of Asian Business and Economics Studies. I led a five-member team through a six-month empirical study.

  • Built binary and ratio-based measures from the Vietnam Household Living Standard Survey (2016 and 2018), covering about 9,300 households in all 63 provinces
  • Logit and panel models found a significant positive association, with clean-energy households showing digital literacy rates 2.5 times higher
  • Heterogeneity analysis showed a stronger urban effect linked to infrastructure gaps, leading to policy recommendations on digital infrastructure and digital literacy in education
  • Stata
  • Logit
  • Panel data
  • Policy recommendations

Investment & Analytics Principles of Valuation · 2023

Vietnam Real Estate Market Report: National, Ho Chi Minh City and Industrial Property

Course report analyzing Vietnam's real estate market from the national picture to Ho Chi Minh City, with a segment study of industrial property covering rents, occupancy and FDI.

Investment & Analytics Team of five · Apr 2023

Optimal Stock Portfolio: Simulation, Optimization and Backtest

Built and tested a stock and treasury bill portfolio for a VND 500 million capital base, starting from macro, industry and company analysis of six listed Vietnamese firms.

  • Simulated 20,000 weight combinations in Excel and used Solver to find the minimum variance and Sharpe-maximizing portfolios
  • Allocated 83.68% to five stocks and 16.32% to treasury bills against a 20% annual return target
  • A one-year in-sample backtest returned 20.06% (VND 100.3 million) while the VN-Index fell about 31%
  • Excel
  • Solver
  • Portfolio optimization
  • Sharpe ratio
